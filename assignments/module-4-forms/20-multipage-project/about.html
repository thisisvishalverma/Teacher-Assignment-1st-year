Learn by making together.

Open Desk is a fictional student club created for this HTML exercise. Its goal is to make practice feel approachable: start with a question, build a small example, and explain what you learned.

Our session rhythm

  1. Pick one small topic.
  2. Try a short activity.
  3. Share one thing that worked and one question.
